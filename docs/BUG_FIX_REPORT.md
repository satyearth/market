## 2. P1 Performance Improvements & P2 Financial Data Quality

### Performance Optimizations
- **64 KB Fast Header Stats Reader (`readMarketStatsHead`):** Replaced synchronous ~23 MB `fs.readFileSync()` calls in `stock-query-service.js` (`readLocalStatsSafe()`) and `local-server.js` (`readMarketStatsFromDataset()`) with bounded 64 KB head chunk reads.
  - Measured (`15` iterations on `23,115,807 B` `js/stocks-data.js`): average read dropped from **`81.714 ms`** to **`4.16 ms`** (**`~19.6x` speedup**).
- **Paginated Payload Stripping:** `executeStockQuery()` now strips `historical_prices` and `description` on list requests (`!exactIsin && !includeHistory`).
  - Measured (`limit=50`, `offset=0`): page JSON size reduced from **`225,904 B`** to **`60,889 B`** (**`3.71x` reduction**).
- **Hoisted Watchlist `Set` & Precomputed Non-Enumerable Search Index:** Removed per-row `new Set(...)` construction (`200`-ISIN watchlist query avg: **`2.847 ms`** across `5,465` rows) and added non-enumerable `_searchIndex` + query-parse caching in `searchMatches()`, resolving `scripts/qa-frontend-audit.js` sub-5ms filter benchmark reliably.

### P2 Financial Coverage, Split Safety & Provenance Metadata
- **Live EOD Price-Scaled Market Caps with Split & Collision Protection:**
  - Verified Screener (`2026-11-04` snapshot) entries in `resolveStockMarketCap()` are checked against the equity's `2026-11-04` historical close (`+-10%` tolerance rejects ticker-collision mismatches like `INE751R01014` `RAJPUTANA` and `INE367E01033` `SEL`) and checked for single-session `<= -35%` post-reference corporate split drops (`hasPostRefSplitDiscontinuity()`, protecting split equities like `PASHUPATI`).
  - For `4,914` verified continuous equities on `2026-11-27`, `total_outstanding_shares` and EOD price-scaled `market_cap_cr` are computed cleanly (`market_cap_as_of_date: "2026-11-27"`), eliminating stale `2026-11-04` Top-343 caps while keeping unverified equities `null`.
- **Non-Fabricated Fundamentals & Explicit Data Provenance:**
  - Wired `screenerEntry` P/E and ROCE into [`src/enrichment/fundamentals.js`](../src/enrichment/fundamentals.js) (`4,306` P/E, `5,237` ROCE, `150` P/B, `137` D/E, `5,301` stocks with `fundamentals_source` & `fundamentals_as_of_date: "2026-11-04"`). No fundamental ratio is ever derived from stock price alone; unavailable ratios remain `null`.
  - Updated [`js/stock-details.js`](../js/stock-details.js) & [`stock.html`](../stock.html) to render `market_cap_as_of_date`, `market_cap_source`, `fundamentals_source`, and `fundamentals_as_of_date`.
  - Fixed misleading `"zero trading volume"` reason on priced Mainboard stocks missing reference market caps in [`src/publishing/publisher.js`](../src/publishing/publisher.js) (`docs/reports/MARKET_CAP_COVERAGE_REPORT.md`).
